MyCellar

MyCellar is an app for keeping track of a personal drinks cellar. You record the wine, whisky and other bottles you own, where each one is stored, what you paid, and how you rate it, so you always know what is in the cellar and where to find it.

What you can do

  • Add drinks with their details, such as the type, producer, region and vintage, plus tasting notes.
  • Record how many bottles you hold and which storage location each one is in, and rename or add locations.
  • Note what you paid and give each drink a rating, using the rating scale you prefer.
  • Search and browse the cellar, and use the cellar management tools to move, clear or remove bottles in bulk.
  • Open your cellar on any device when you sign in.

Access

MyCellar is a small personal project and is currently invite-only. You sign in with your Google account, so there is no separate password to manage.

Sign-in and your information

When you sign in, MyCellar receives only your name, your email address and an account identifier from Google. It does not get access to your email, files, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Read the MyCellar privacy policy for what is stored and how to delete it.
