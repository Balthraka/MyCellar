MyCellar privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What MyCellar is

MyCellar is a small, invite-only app for keeping track of a personal drinks cellar. This page explains what information it handles.

What MyCellar collects

  • From Google sign-in: your name, your email address and a unique account identifier. MyCellar asks for nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you enter: your drinks and their details, tasting notes, ratings, prices, bottle counts, storage locations and your settings.
  • A sign-in token: after you sign in, a random token is saved in your browser so you stay signed in. It expires after 7 days or when you sign out. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.

What MyCellar does not do

  • It does not sell or rent your information.
  • It does not show ads and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It does not read your Google email, files or contacts.
  • It does not send you email.

Look up details

The app has an optional Look up details button. When you press it, the drink's type, name and producer are sent to an artificial-intelligence service run by Cloudflare (Workers AI) to suggest facts such as region and grape. The text sent and the answer are not kept. MyCellar stores only a daily count of how many look-ups you have used, which is limited to 20 a day, and removes old counts. Nothing else from your cellar is sent.

How the information is used

Your name and email identify your account. Your cellar is stored so it is there when you sign in on another device. Nothing is used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored and who else is involved

  • Your account and cellar are stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare. The app itself is served from GitHub Pages.
  • Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare also runs the AI service used by Look up details, as described above.
  • To display the app, your browser loads code libraries from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services can see your IP address when your browser asks them for these files. MyCellar does not send them anything about you or your cellar.

Who can see it

Your cellar is private to your account. The person who runs MyCellar could technically see stored data when maintaining the service, and will not share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

Backups

The person who runs MyCellar keeps occasional backups of the database. Backups are replaced over time, so data you delete may remain in a backup for a limited period before it is removed.

Deleting your data

In the app, open your account and choose Delete my account. This permanently deletes your cellar, your sign-in details and your account from the MyCellar database. If you cannot use the app, ask the person who invited you to MyCellar to delete your data.

Contact

MyCellar is invite-only. For questions about this policy, ask the person who invited you to the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
